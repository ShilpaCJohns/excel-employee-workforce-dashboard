# 📊 Advanced Employee Workforce Dashboard (Excel & Pivot Tables)

A comprehensive, professional-grade **HR Analytics & Workforce Dashboard** built entirely in Microsoft Excel using raw employee data, advanced pivot tables, dynamic calculations, and interactive visual charts. This project is designed to provide HR leaders and management with clear, real-time insights into headcount dynamics, attrition risks, workforce diversity, and performance ratings.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Visuals Included

* **Executive Summary KPIs:** Real-time tracking of Total Employees (785), Active Employees (561), Attrition Count (224), Attrition Rate (28.5%), Average Tenure (5.1 Years), and Gender Diversity Ratio (59% / 41%).
* **Departmental & Location Analytics:** 
  * Headcount distribution by department (Operations, Sales, IT, Finance, Marketing, HR).
  * Regional headcount analysis spanning major hubs (Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Pune, Delhi).
* **Trends & Attrition Insights:** 
  * Monthly Headcount Trend trajectory.
  * Department-wise attrition percentages to pinpoint retention risk zones.
  * Active Headcount vs. Attrition comparison tracking.
* **Performance & Job Level Matrix:** Performance rating distribution across the organization mapped alongside entry, mid-level, and senior job hierarchies.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic multi-parameter filtering by Department, Location, Job Level, Year, and Month to slice data on the fly.

---

## 📂 Workbook Structure

The Excel workbook (`Data (4).xlsx`) is organized into three primary sheets:
1. **`Dashboard`**: The front-end interactive dashboard housing KPI cards, interactive slicers, and formatted charts.
2. **`Pivot_Table`**: The intermediate analytical processing layer utilizing Excel Pivot Tables to aggregate data dynamically.
3. **`Employee_Data`**: The underlying raw dataset containing comprehensive employee records (demographics, hire/exit dates, tenure, salaries, performance ratings, attendance, and employment status).

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used

* **Microsoft Excel**: Advanced Formulas, Data Modeling, and Conditional Formatting.
* **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts**: Dynamic data aggregation and cross-tabulation.
* **HR Data Analytics**: Workforce planning metrics, attrition tracking, tenure calculation, and diversity indexing.

## ⚙️ How to View/Use Locally

1. **Clone or Download** this repository to your local machine.

3. Ensure content/macros are enabled if prompted, and use the **Slicers** at the top of the `Dashboard` sheet to filter and interact with the workforce metrics dynamically.
